Questions

Is it really free?

Yes, for you. We never charge candidates. Like recruitment firms, we're paid by the companies that hire through us, and that fee never comes out of your salary.

Who can register?

Anyone who has served in the Indian Army, Indian Navy or Indian Air Force and has already left service: SSC officers, retired officers, JCOs, NCOs and other ranks. We don't register serving personnel.

Do you guarantee a job?

No. We help you present yourself well and apply properly, and we refer where there's a genuine fit. The hiring decision is always the company's.

Do mentors get paid?

Yes. We pay our mentors for their time. Once you register, we'll reach out to discuss how it works and agree the details with you.

Do I have to take a mentor?

No. Mentorship is optional, and only for those who ask for it. You can use our other services, like referrals, job openings and help with your CV, without it.

Do you verify mentors?

Yes, as far as we can online. Before any mentor joins, we review their LinkedIn profile and meet them on a video call (Zoom or Google Meet) to understand their experience and why they want to mentor. We don't run formal background checks, so please use your own judgement too. The list of mentors we send you includes each mentor's NextPosting ID, and we never pass your contact details to a mentor without your consent.

What if something goes wrong?

We have a zero-tolerance policy on inappropriate behaviour. Everyone who registers gets a unique ID. If anyone you've been connected with through NextPosting behaves in a way that concerns you, email us at with their NextPosting ID. We'll look into it straight away, and anyone found to have behaved inappropriately is removed from the platform immediately.

Are the job opportunities genuine?

Yes. We only share openings from credible sources, such as company career pages, established job portals and hiring contacts in our network. If anyone asks you to pay a fee to apply for a job in our name, it didn't come from us.

What happens after I register?

We'll contact you on WhatsApp or email. We're a small team, so it can take a few days.

What if I forget my NextPosting ID?

Email us at from the email address you registered with, and we'll send it to you.

What do you do with my details?

We use them only to help with your job search, and we never sell them. To have them deleted, email us at with your NextPosting ID.To have them deleted, just let us know when we contact you.

How mentoring works

  1. Tell us what you need help with in this form.
  2. We shortlist mentors who match your needs and email you the list.
  3. You pick the mentor you'd like.
  4. With your consent, we connect you both by sharing the relevant contact details.
  5. You connect the way you prefer: email, phone, Zoom or Google Meet.

About our mentors

We review every mentor's LinkedIn profile and meet them on a video call before they join. We don't run formal background checks, so please use your own judgement too.
