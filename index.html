Free. No fees, no placement charges.

The experience is there. The translation isn't.

Years of leading teams, running logistics and managing people under pressure don't come across well on a corporate CV. Most people leaving the forces have never written one for a recruiter, never used job portals properly, and don't know how referrals actually work. That's the gap we're here to close.

  • CVs written for the wrong reader

    Service records list postings and appointments. Recruiters look for scope, numbers and results, in their own language.

  • Applying without matching

    Sending the same CV to fifty openings rarely works. Each application has to reflect the job description.

  • No one to ask inside

    Most corporate hiring runs on referrals, and most people leaving service don't know who to ask.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes, for you. We will never charge candidates. In future, companies that hire through us may pay us a fee, the way recruitment firms work. That fee never comes out of your salary.

Who can register?

Anyone serving or who has served in the Army, Navy or Air Force and is planning a corporate career: SSC officers, officers taking premature retirement, JCOs, NCOs and other ranks.

Do you guarantee a job?

No. We help you present yourself well and apply properly, and we refer where there's a genuine fit. The hiring decision is always the company's.

Do mentors get paid?

No. Mentoring is pro bono for now. We're building the community first, and our mentors give their time because they want to see people from the forces do well.

Do you verify mentors?

No. We don't vet or verify mentor profiles at the moment. We share a list of available mentors and suggest matches, but we haven't checked their backgrounds, so please use your own judgement before sharing anything personal. We never pass your contact details to a mentor without your consent.

Are the job opportunities genuine?

Yes. We only share openings from credible sources, such as company career pages, established job portals and hiring contacts in our network. If anyone asks you to pay a fee to apply for a job in our name, it didn't come from us.

What happens after I register?

We'll contact you on WhatsApp or email. We're a small team, so it can take a few days.

What do you do with my details?

We use them only to help with your job search, and we never sell them. To have them deleted, email us at .To have them deleted, just let us know when we contact you.
